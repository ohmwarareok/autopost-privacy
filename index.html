About this app

AutoPost is a private tool used only by its owner to publish video Reels and comments to Facebook Pages that the owner manages, and to read those Pages' own performance statistics. It is not offered to the public, and no other users sign in to it.

Data we access

  • The list of Facebook Pages the owner manages, and their Page access tokens, to publish Reels and comments.
  • Page insights (views, engagements, follows) for those Pages, to see which content performs well.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to post the owner's own content and to show the owner reports about their own Pages. It is stored only on the owner's personal computer. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data about other people

AutoPost does not collect personal data about people who view or comment on the Pages.

Data deletion

To remove AutoPost's access, go to Facebook Settings → Security and Login → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites), select AutoPost and click Remove. To have locally stored tokens and reports deleted, email the owner at the address below. Deletion is completed within 7 days.
